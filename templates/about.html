About COR AMANS

A Catholic marriage formation platform rooted in doctrine, prayer, and pastoral care.

COR AMANS helps engaged couples prepare for marriage through faithful Catholic teaching, guided formation, live meetings, and practical steps that support sacramental readiness and lifelong vocation.

Mission

What COR AMANS is designed to do

COR AMANS provides a faithful and secure Catholic marriage preparation experience for engaged couples. The platform brings together doctrinal teaching, pastoral guidance, live classes, and couple-centered reflection in one structured journey.

The curriculum follows the Church's teaching on matrimony as covenant and sacrament, supporting couples as they grow in prayer, mutual love, fidelity, and readiness for the vocation of family life.

The goal is not only to deliver information, but to help couples build a strong spiritual and practical foundation for a Christ-centered marriage.

Core emphasis

Catholic doctrine on marriage

Sacrament and covenant

Marriage is presented as a lifelong covenant raised by Christ to the dignity of a sacrament.

Domestic church

Couples are prepared to build homes shaped by prayer, charity, witness, and communion.

Pastoral support

Live meetings and mentor guidance help couples apply doctrine in real, everyday married life.

Faith-based guidance

All lessons and live sessions are aligned with Catholic doctrine and sacramental preparation.

Secure learning environment

User access, dashboard workflow, and account protection are designed with modern security in mind.

Couples support

Mentor circles, classroom prompts, and practical resources help couples prepare together.
